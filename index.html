$DARK Token

The node keeps burning.

Every Dark Node NFT carries a share of scheduled $DARK emissions, weighted by its series. Rarer nodes mine more; the Golden Oracle 1/1s carry the heaviest weight. When an NFT changes hands, past accrual stays claimable by the previous holder and future accrual moves to the new owner.
